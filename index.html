💵 Basics
  • 🪙 You bet with the fake currency you're given — no real money involved.
  • 📧 To join, email or talk to the commissioner.
  • 🚫 Mess with the slides and you'll be permanently banned.
  • 🏈 Football only.
  • 📅 New bets every week.
⏰ Deadlines
  • 🔒 All bets lock Fridays at 7:00 PM before Indian Creek kickoff.
  • 📝 You must place at least 2 bets per week or you lose $10,000.
  • 💸 Each bet has a minimum entry amount listed on the bet card.
🎯 Special Rules
  • ⚔️ Rival Week (Rule 10): Any rival game has a ×2 payout.
  • 🏠 Home Sweet Home (Rule 11): Get 3 home games right in a row → win $15,000.
  • 🌟 Perfect Saturday (Rule 13): Get 5 bets right in a week → win $50,000.
  • 🎯 Exact Score (Rule 14): Guess the exact score → get ×5 your bet amount.
